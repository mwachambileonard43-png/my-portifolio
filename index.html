Our Story

Leo Graphics Media was founded by Leonard James Mwachambi during December 2024, at Dar es salaam institute of Technology (DIT) in Dar es salaam Tanzania.

Leo Graphics Media was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to transform businesses through exceptional visual design. We are a creative design studio passionate about bringing your visual ideas to life with precision, creativity, and professional excellence.

With years of experience in graphic design, branding, and digital media, we specialize in creating compelling visual solutions that help businesses stand out in today's competitive market. Our team combines artistic creativity with strategic thinking to deliver designs that not only look amazing but also drive real business results.
